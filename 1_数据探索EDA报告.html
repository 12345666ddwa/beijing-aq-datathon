6. Box-Cox λ 测定

  • 数据前提: 目标全 >0(min=2),无需 log(y+c) 平移,可直接 Box-Cox: y(λ) = (y^λ − 1)/λ
  • 全样本(360,954 点): scipy.stats.boxcox_normmax method='mle'(最大化变换后正态似然)→ λ = 0.1668;method='pearsonr'(最大化变换值与正态分位数相关)→ λ = 0.1905
  • 逐站 λ(mle): 范围 0.0974(Dingling) → 0.1995(Dongsi),12 站平均 0.1639:
站点 λ 站点 λ
Aotizhongxin 0.1573 Changping
Dingling 0.0974 Dongsi
Guanyuan 0.1652 Gucheng
Huairou 0.1493 Nongzhanguan
Shunyi 0.1655 Tiantan
Wanliu 0.1886 Wanshouxigong

与队友参考图(R fable Guerrero λ≈0.0895)的方法差异(如实说明): - 队友图来自 R fable/forecast 的 Guerrero(1993)估计器:以方差稳定为目标,在季节窗口上最小化分段标准差(去季节化子序列)的变异系数,与 fable::box_cox 默认行为一致。 - 本报告 scipy 的两个估计器目标不同: mle 假设变换后近似正态、最大化 profile 似然;pearsonr 最大化变换值与正态分位数(QQ)相关。三者在同一数量级(Guerrero 0.09 vs scipy mle 0.167 / pearsonr 0.191),结论一致:强烈压缩右偏,接近但不完全等于对数(λ=0)。 - 工程建议: λ∈[0.09, 0.19] 区间内,log1p(λ=0) 与 Box-Cox(λ=0.17) 的预测差异在 RMSE 尺度上通常很小;比赛按原始尺度评分,反变换(Duan 或直接 exp 后加残差调整)环节的取舍比 λ 的精确值更重要。可做 raw vs log vs boxcox 三组实验维度(对应 train_rf.py 的 --transform 开关)。

8. 关键发现(供模型决策)

  1. 目标右偏 + 封顶: mean 78.0 > median 55,skew 2.01,max=999 仅 1 行;RMSE 对高值事件敏感 → 值得做目标变换实验(raw / log1p / Box-Cox λ≈0.17),评分在原始尺度,反变换策略要单独验证。
  2. λ 测定结论: 全样本 scipy mle λ=0.167、pearsonr λ=0.191,逐站 0.097–0.199,与 R fable Guerrero 0.0895 同向(方法目标不同,如实记录)→ 接近对数变换但偏轻压缩。
  3. 主导信号 = 现时污染物: PM10 r=0.848 / CO 0.762 / NO2 0.643;线性基线(无滞后)RMSE 36.1,比常数基线低约 64.6%。特征工程重心应放在污染物交互(如 PM10×CO、二次项、站点×污染物)上。
  4. 日历特征要防年际漂移: 验证期(供暖季)与训练期同月均值系统性偏移(2015-12、2016-02 均创极端)→ month 类特征可用但模型必须主要靠实时特征;时间切分验证(而非随机 K 折)是唯一可信评估方式,test 与 train 无缝衔接(2016-08-31 23:00 起),后期可在 2016-03→08 时段做二次 holdout 检查泛化。
  5. 缺失与站点: 缺失集中在 CO/O3/NO2/SO2(1–4.4%),训练期中位数插补足够起步,后续可试按站点插补或加缺失指示列;站点均值差约 18 μg/m³(83.7 vs 65.2),one-hot 起步,后续可试按站建模对比。

6. Box-Cox λ Estimation

  • Data premise: the target is entirely > 0 (min = 2), so no log(y+c) shift is needed; Box-Cox can be applied directly: y(λ) = (y^λ − 1)/λ
  • Full sample (360,954 points): scipy.stats.boxcox_normmax with method='mle' (maximizes the transformed normality likelihood) → λ = 0.1668; method='pearsonr' (maximizes the correlation between transformed values and normal quantiles) → λ = 0.1905
  • Per-station λ (mle): range 0.0974 (Dingling) → 0.1995 (Dongsi), mean over the 12 stations 0.1639:
Station λ Station λ
Aotizhongxin 0.1573 Changping
Dingling 0.0974 Dongsi
Guanyuan 0.1652 Gucheng
Huairou 0.1493 Nongzhanguan
Shunyi 0.1655 Tiantan
Wanliu 0.1886 Wanshouxigong

Methodological differences vs. teammate's reference figure (R fable Guerrero λ≈0.0895), reported honestly: - The teammate's figure comes from R fable/forecast's Guerrero (1993) estimator: it targets variance stabilization, minimizing over seasonal windows the coefficient of variation of piecewise standard deviations (of deseasonalized subsequences), consistent with fable::box_cox default behavior. - The two scipy estimators in this report have different objectives: mle assumes the transformed data is approximately normal and maximizes the profile likelihood; pearsonr maximizes the correlation between transformed values and normal (QQ) quantiles. The three are of the same order of magnitude (Guerrero 0.09 vs scipy mle 0.167 / pearsonr 0.191) and agree on the conclusion: strong compression of the right skew, close to but not exactly the log transform (λ = 0). - Engineering recommendation: within the λ∈[0.09, 0.19] interval, the difference between log1p (λ=0) and Box-Cox (λ=0.17) predictions is usually small on the RMSE scale; since the competition scores on the original scale, the choice of inverse transform (Duan smearing, or exp followed by residual adjustment) matters more than the exact value of λ. Run raw vs log vs boxcox as three experimental dimensions (corresponding to the --transform switch in train_rf.py).

8. Key Findings (for modeling decisions)

  1. Right-skewed + capped target: mean 78.0 > median 55, skew 2.01, max = 999 with only 1 row; RMSE is sensitive to high-value events → target transform experiments are worthwhile (raw / log1p / Box-Cox λ≈0.17); scoring is on the original scale, so the inverse-transform strategy must be validated separately.
  2. λ estimation conclusion: full-sample scipy mle λ = 0.167, pearsonr λ = 0.191, per-station 0.097–0.199, consistent in direction with the R fable Guerrero 0.0895 (different method objectives, recorded honestly) → close to log transform but with lighter compression.
  3. Dominant signal = current pollutants: PM10 r = 0.848 / CO 0.762 / NO2 0.643; linear baseline (no lags) RMSE 36.1, ≈64.6% lower than the constant baseline. Feature engineering should focus on pollutant interactions (e.g., PM10×CO, quadratic terms, station × pollutant).
  4. Calendar features must guard against inter-annual drift: same-month means in the validation period (heating season) are systematically shifted from the training period (both 2015-12 and 2016-02 set extremes) → month-type features are usable, but the model must rely mainly on real-time features; time-aware split validation (not random K-fold) is the only credible evaluation scheme. Test and train are seamless (starting 2016-08-31 23:00), so a secondary holdout over 2016-03 → 08 can be run later to check generalization.
  5. Missingness & stations: missingness concentrates in CO/O3/NO2/SO2 (1–4.4%); train-period median imputation suffices to start, with per-station imputation or missing-indicator columns as later options; station means differ by ≈ 18 μg/m³ (83.7 vs 65.2) — one-hot to start, per-station modeling as a later comparison.
